# Findings Log

Running notes on results as the analysis develops. Each entry: what I ran, what I found, what it means.

## Finding 1: First look (Phase 4)

- Jan–Jun 2024 → 2025: into-zone +2.7%, outside +7.9% (gap −5.2 pts).
- Stable stations only (existed all of Jan 2024 – Dec 2025): into-zone +0.2%, outside +5.3% (gap −5.1 pts) → station expansion doesn't explain the gap.
- Monthly ratio (into-zone trips per 100 outside trips) is strongly seasonal (~11 in winter, ~15 in summer); the gap is concentrated in Jan–Feb 2025. Jul–Sep 2025 were actually above 2024.
- Full year: into-zone +1.7%, outside +3.1% (gap −1.4 pts).
- Weather check: Jan–Feb 2025 averaged ~5°F colder and snowier than Jan–Feb 2024 (Jan 2024 was rainier). Into-zone trips are more weather-sensitive than outside trips, so weather plausibly explains most of the Jan–Feb dip.
- **Working conclusion:** no clear sustained increase in bike trips into the zone after tolling began. To be tested formally with weather and season controls in Phase 5.
